## Sources

- **Table of contents:** three sources agree on all chapter numbers and titles: the Goodreads History Book Club syllabus (with page numbers), archive.org catalog metadata, and the Bookey chapter summaries. The only disagreement was the title of chapter 16: archive.org has "The glorious game", while the other two have "The Glorious Cause". Going with "The Glorious Cause".
- **Chapter contents:** taken from Bookey's per-chapter summaries. Many of them cut off partway through (behind a paywall), and they contain small factual errors, e.g. they date the Annapolis resignation to Dec 4 instead of Dec 23. Where the summaries don't settle where an event belongs, the mapping is inferred from chronology and the page ranges below, and marked **⚠ verify**.
- **Scope:** Part Three "The General" (chs. 16–37, pp. 181–457). Lexington, Concord and Bunker Hill appear in ch. 16 as background.
